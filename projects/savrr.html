Overview

Savour the Taste, Save the Effort

SAVRR is a conceptual smart cooking system designed to simplify and enrich the kitchen experience for young, time-pressed individuals. The app concept enables easy and intuitive control of multiple cooking hobs, supporting hobbyists from beginner to intermediate levels who want to cook efficiently.

Assuming the presence of compatible IoT technology, the project focused on exploring and testing the mobile user interface that bridges the gap between smart control systems and real cooking experiences.

Grounded in extensive user research and behavioral analysis, the design evolved into an interface structured around two core user journeys. The process combined usability heuristics, Fitts’s Law, and iterative testing to create a seamless and responsive cooking experience.

Problem

User research (desk studies, online survey of 32 participants, five direct observations and interview) highlighted the following problem areas.

Notably along with key frustrations, 78.1% of respondents reported the experience of undercooking or burning, highlighting the need for more supportive cooking interface.

Managing Multiple Cooking Tasks
Users found it stressful to coordinate several hobs simultaneously, often losing track of timing and sequence.
Easy and Seamless Control
Existing digital appliances lacked an intuitive way to manage precision in temperature and timing. Users wanted easy yet reliable control.
Adapting to Real-time Changes
Unexpected interruptions required quick reactions, but most interfaces didn’t support flexible two-directional intervention.
USER JOURNEY Defined two main user personas and journeys to focus on for the MVP:

1. Confidence Builder: users seeking to gain control over multiple tasks and cooking stages.
2. Effort Saver: users wanting consistent, recipe-based results with minimal monitoring.
SYSTEM FLOW Developed structural flow prioritizing hierarchical control over conditional steps. Each interaction was mapped to possible IoT input/output points, ensuring logical consistency and future scalability for smart appliance integration.
REAL WORLD MATCH Reflected real cooking interactions in the interface through intuitive visual metaphors — color-coded zones, clear layout hierarchy, and a hob activation flow mirroring actual stovetop operation.
ERROR PREVENTION Integrated design constraints, control depth limits, and confirmation prompts to prevent overheating or mistimed operations — reducing user errors without restricting freedom.
FITT'S LAW Divided controls by frequency of use into easy, medium, and hard zones. Common controls were positioned for quick reach, with shortcuts embedded in the most accessible areas to minimize interaction time during busy cooking moments.
