How might we reposition an architecture firm for commercial credibility? Reframing an architecture practice from expression-led to client-oriented.

Overview

Heta Architects is an innovative architecture firm in London with projects spanning residential, commercial, industrial, and masterplanning sectors. As a User Experience Design Intern, I led end-to-end design initiatives during a pivotal phase of the firm’s strategic brand repositioning.

My role extended beyond visuals and UI/UX into strategic brand repositioning. While Heta had broad portfolio scope, its digital presence did not reflect that diversity. The previous website prioritized visual expression and creative approaches, which left the firm’s service capabilities and strategic expertise underrepresented.

Over 5 months, I explored and redefined how Heta communicates its value through a structured, research-driven redesign process. Key outcomes included the introduction of a structured user research framework, delivery of a new neutral website, and creation of internal brand assets that shaped how Heta communicates both internally and externally.

Problem

AS-IS
  • Visual storytelling, Creative and insight-led approaches to the architecture

  • Landing page dominated by imagery, especially the tone of the first project image, limited visibility into the firm’s full service capability or strategic thinking
TO-BE
  • Client-focused, capability-driven platform

  • Reflect the firm’s breadth across multiple sectors in a balanced way

  • Emphasise services, expertises, and strategic approach beyond portfolio listing

  • Establish a neutral and consistent brand design language
FLOW RESTRUCTURE Before the design of the website, I revised the flow within the website. In the process, I revised the content management system (CMS), reducing over 10 collections down to 4 core structures. By improving scalability, it allowed the team to update project information efficiently without technical bottlenecks.
